EPICYCLE samples
================
Drop any PCM WAV into Epicycle/samples/ on the card — any filename.

Pads boot EMPTY. No pad01.wav / 1.wav auto-map, no demo kit preload.
On the SAMPLE page, browse by filename, preview, and assign to a pad
(encoder push). SAMPLE+pad selects the target pad.

PCM WAV only (16/24/32-bit or float). Mono or stereo.
Empty pad press = silence until you assign a WAV.

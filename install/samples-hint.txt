EPICYCLE samples
================
Copy WAV files to Epicycle/samples/ on the card:

  pad01.wav … pad16.wav
  (or 1.wav … 16.wav)

PCM WAV only (16/24/32-bit or float). Mono or stereo.
If no files are found, EPICYCLE uses a built-in demo tone bank so you can
still hear synced 4-beat loops and 3-vs-5 polymeter on pads 1–4.

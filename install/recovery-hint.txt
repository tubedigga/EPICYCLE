EPICYCLE recovery (card-level — no firmware flash)

Hang at boot:
  Hold POWER until the unit powers off. Remove the microSD card. Boot stock.

Bad build:
  Rename the card's "autorun" folder to "autorun-off" (or delete it), then boot.
  With SampleBoot: encoder → stock or a previous mod folder.

App crash mid-session:
  The launcher should restart ac50.service (stock). If not, power-cycle without
  the card, or rename autorun as above.

User projects and samples live in Epicycle/ on the card root and are not
replaced when you overwrite autorun/.
